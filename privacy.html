Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Working draft: This policy describes the intended privacy approach for WorldLink Business. Before public launch, we must verify our actual data practices, identify the responsible organization, establish a privacy contact, and review applicable law.

1. Introduction

WorldLink Business is being developed as a global business directory and marketplace. This policy explains the categories of information the platform may handle and how that information is intended to be used.

The final policy must identify the organization or person legally responsible for deciding how personal information is processed.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, information may include:

  • Business name and business contact details.
  • Country, city, and business location.
  • Business category and description.
  • Business website address.
  • Messages, enquiries, or complaints submitted to the platform.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the service.

We should not collect additional personal information unless there is a clear, lawful, and disclosed reason for doing so.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Creating and displaying business directory listings.
  • Operating business registration features.
  • Responding to enquiries and complaints.
  • Maintaining and improving website functionality.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any notice or consent required by law.

4. Public Business Listings

Information submitted for a public business listing may be visible to visitors, search engines, and other internet users.

Before submitting information, business owners should consider whether the details are suitable for public display. They should avoid including private personal information that is not necessary for their business listing.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, WorldLink Business must identify and document an appropriate basis for each relevant purpose.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a basis may include consent, performance of a contract, a legal obligation, or another legally recognized ground.

6. Sharing Information

Information may need to be shared with service providers that help operate the website, such as hosting providers, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

Information may also be disclosed where required or authorized by law. The final policy must accurately identify relevant service providers and other recipients once they are selected.

We should not sell personal information or share it for unrelated purposes without a lawful basis and the disclosures or consent required by applicable law.

7. Data Storage and Security

WorldLink Business should use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Actual safeguards, access controls, backup arrangements, and incident response procedures must be implemented and tested rather than merely promised in this policy.

8. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its disclosed purposes, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

The final policy must explain the retention periods or criteria that actually apply to different categories of information.

9. Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on the laws that apply, individuals may have rights to request access to their personal information, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a relevant authority.

The availability and scope of these rights depend on applicable law and the circumstances. WorldLink Business must establish a working process for receiving and responding to privacy requests.

10. Cookies and Technical Data

The website's actual use of cookies, local storage, analytics, and similar technologies must be reviewed before launch.

If such technologies are used, this policy should explain their purposes and describe any choices available to users. Any required consent must be obtained before the relevant technology is used.

11. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for business-related use, not for collecting children's personal information. Any age restrictions and safeguards must comply with the laws applicable to the users and services involved.

12. International Data Transfers

Because WorldLink Business is intended to serve users in different countries, information may be processed in countries other than the country where it was collected.

Before launch, the platform must identify relevant hosting and service-provider locations and implement any transfer safeguards required by applicable law.

13. Third-Party Websites

Business listings may contain links to external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices, which users should review independently.

14. Changes to This Policy

This policy may need to change as the platform develops. Material changes should be explained clearly, dated, and communicated where required by applicable law.

15. Contact and Complaints

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, and complaints, contact WorldLink Business at worldlinkbusinessglobal@gmail.com. This contact channel should be monitored and requests handled under applicable data protection requirements.

Users should also be informed of any relevant data protection authority or complaint procedure required by applicable law.

16. Important Notice

This document is a working draft and does not itself guarantee legal compliance or establish that every described process has already been implemented. The final policy must reflect the platform's actual operations and be reviewed against applicable legal requirements before public launch.
