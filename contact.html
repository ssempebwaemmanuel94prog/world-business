Contact and Complaints

WorldLink Business aims to provide a useful and trustworthy environment for discovering businesses and commercial opportunities. This page explains how to prepare a request for assistance or report a problem.

Contact: You can reach WorldLink Business at worldlinkbusinessglobal@gmail.com. This email opens your email application; it does not submit a website form.

1. General Enquiries

For questions about the directory, business registration, listing information, or platform features, prepare a message describing your question and the assistance you need.

Email: worldlinkbusinessglobal@gmail.com

2. Report a Business or Listing

You may need to report a listing that:

  • Contains false or misleading information.
  • Appears to impersonate another business.
  • Advertises suspected fraudulent activity.
  • Offers unlawful or dangerous products or services.
  • Uses photographs, logos, or other content without permission.
  • Appears to violate the marketplace rules.

3. What to Include in a Complaint

When preparing a complaint, include relevant details such as:

  • The name of the business or listing involved.
  • The listing address or reference number, if available.
  • A clear description of the concern.
  • The date you noticed the problem.
  • Relevant evidence that you are authorized to share.
  • A safe way to contact you if a response is necessary.

Do not include passwords, bank PINs, payment card security codes, or unnecessary sensitive personal information in a complaint.

4. Transaction Disputes

If you have a disagreement with a business about a purchase, delivery, payment, or refund, keep your transaction records and contact the business through a channel you can independently verify.

WorldLink Business's role in individual transactions will depend on the services actually provided by the platform. Do not assume that the platform processes payments or guarantees refunds unless those services and protections have been expressly established.

5. Safety and Urgent Matters

If you believe someone is in immediate danger or a crime is occurring, contact the appropriate local emergency service or law-enforcement authority. Do not rely on this website for emergency assistance.

6. Privacy

Share only the information needed to explain your concern. Please review our Privacy Policy for information about the intended handling of personal data. Actual practices must be confirmed before public launch.
