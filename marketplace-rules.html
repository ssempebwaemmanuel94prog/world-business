Marketplace Rules

Last updated: 10 October 2026

These rules are a working draft for the developing WorldLink Business platform. They must be adapted to the marketplace features actually offered before public launch.

1. Our Marketplace Principles

WorldLink Business aims to help people discover legitimate businesses, products, services, and commercial opportunities. Users are expected to act honestly, lawfully, and respectfully.

2. Accurate Listings

Sellers and business owners must:

  • Use accurate business names and contact information.
  • Describe products and services honestly.
  • State prices and additional charges clearly when applicable.
  • Disclose important limitations and relevant conditions.
  • Use genuine photographs or images they are authorized to use.
  • Correct or remove listings that become inaccurate.

3. Prohibited Listings

Listings involving unlawful goods or services are prohibited. The platform must also prohibit content that applicable law forbids and may restrict additional categories to protect users.

Prohibited listings include, as applicable:

  • Counterfeit products and goods that infringe intellectual property.
  • Stolen goods or property offered without authorization.
  • Fraudulent investment offers and deceptive financial schemes.
  • Illegal drugs and unlawfully supplied medicines.
  • Weapons or other regulated items offered in violation of applicable law.
  • Human trafficking, exploitation, or services facilitating abuse.
  • Products or services that are illegal in the relevant jurisdiction.
  • Malware, stolen credentials, or services intended for unlawful access.
  • Misleading health claims or dangerous products marketed unlawfully.
  • Any other goods or services prohibited by the platform's published policies.

4. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Users must not impersonate another person or business, fabricate credentials, falsify reviews, manipulate listing information, or make deceptive claims about products, services, or transactions.

Requests for payment must be genuine and accurately described. Users should be cautious of unusual payment requests and promises that appear too good to be true.

5. Respect for Intellectual Property

Users must have the necessary rights or permissions to publish photographs, logos, descriptions, trademarks, and other materials. Content that unlawfully infringes another party's rights may be restricted or removed following appropriate review.

6. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers should:

  • Review business details and product descriptions carefully.
  • Confirm prices, delivery arrangements, and transaction conditions.
  • Check relevant licences or qualifications where appropriate.
  • Use reasonable precautions before sending money.
  • Keep appropriate records of orders and communications.
  • Report suspected fraud or dangerous listings.

7. Seller Responsibilities

Sellers should:

  • Communicate honestly with prospective customers.
  • Fulfil commitments they accept.
  • Explain relevant delivery, cancellation, and refund conditions.
  • Respect applicable consumer protection and product safety laws.
  • Protect personal information received from customers.

8. Business Verification

Unless WorldLink Business explicitly states otherwise, appearing in the directory does not mean that a business has been independently verified, certified, or endorsed.

Verification badges or similar claims must not be displayed unless a real verification process has been implemented and completed.

9. Reviews and Ratings

If reviews or ratings are introduced, they must not be fabricated, manipulated through deceptive methods, or used to threaten or harass other users.

Any review system should have clear procedures for reporting abusive content and addressing credible complaints.

10. Reporting Problems

Users should report suspected scams, unlawful listings, impersonation, dangerous products, and other violations through the official reporting channel published by WorldLink Business.

A monitored reporting channel must be established before the platform relies on this policy for public reporting.

11. Reviews and Enforcement

Subject to applicable law, WorldLink Business may investigate reports and restrict, suspend, or remove listings that violate these rules or present a credible legal, safety, or security risk.

Decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances, and an appropriate review or appeal process should be provided where required by law or adopted by the platform.

12. Transactions and Disputes

Before marketplace transactions are enabled, WorldLink Business must clearly explain its role, if any, in payments, delivery, cancellations, returns, refunds, and dispute resolution.

Users must not assume that the platform guarantees a transaction unless a specific guarantee has been expressly established and its conditions have been published.

13. Changes to These Rules

These rules may change as marketplace features develop. Updates should be dated and communicated to users when required by law.

14. Related Policies

Please also review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

15. Important Notice

These rules are a working draft and do not guarantee that every listed enforcement or reporting feature is currently available. They must be finalized to reflect the actual platform and applicable legal requirements before public launch.
